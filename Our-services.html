⏱️ How Our Group Sessions Work

🕐

1-Hour Sessions

Each session includes focused teaching followed by 15 minutes of dedicated Q&A, giving pupils time to ask questions and clarify anything they are unsure about.

📧

Weekly Topic Updates

Every Sunday, pupils receive an email confirming the topic and content that will be covered in the upcoming sessions.

🔗

Easy Online Access

The session link is sent to pupils 1 hour before the lesson begins, making it easy to join on time.

🎥

Missed a Session?

All tutoring sessions, including the Q&A, are recorded and uploaded to our Video Library, allowing pupils to catch up or revisit lessons whenever they need to.

📈

Regular Check-Ups

Regular progress check-ups are available to help identify areas for improvement and ensure pupils are getting the support they need.

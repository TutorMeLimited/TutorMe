Admin FAQ

Welcome to the Admin FAQ. Here are some quick answers to common dashboard tasks.

How do I grant a student access to the videos?

Go to the Dashboard and click "Generate New Invite Code". Click the Copy button next to it and send it to the student. They will enter this code on the Sign Up page.

Show me

Can an invite code be used multiple times?

No. Each invite code is valid for a single use. Once a student creates an account with it, the code is marked as "Used" and cannot be redeemed again.

How do I temporarily block a user?

In the Manage Users section, click the "Suspend" button next to the user. They will instantly lose access to the platform. You can click "Unsuspend" at any time to restore their access.

Show me

How do I add a new video?

In the Videos section on the Dashboard, paste either the full YouTube URL (e.g. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ) or just the ID (dQw4w9WgXcQ) and click Add Video.

Show me

How do I remove a video?

In the Videos section on your Dashboard, simply click the "Remove" button next to the video link you want to take down. It will instantly disappear from the students' page.

Show me

What if a student forgets their password?

You don't need to do anything! Direct them to the Client Sign In page and have them click the "Forgot password?" link. They can securely reset it themselves via email.

Show me

Can I accidentally suspend or delete the other admin?

No, the system has built-in protections. Admin accounts are clearly labeled with a blue "Admin" badge, and the Suspend/Delete buttons are hidden for those accounts to prevent any accidental lockouts.

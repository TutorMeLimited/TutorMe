Help & FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions by our students and parents.

How do I access the educational videos?

Once you have been provided with an invite code by your tutor, you can sign up for an account. After signing in, click on the "Customer Portal" tab at the top of the page, and select "Educational Videos".

How do I book a tutoring session?

Navigate to the "Bookings" page using the top menu. There you will find instructions and contact forms to arrange either 1-to-1 or group tutoring sessions with our expert teachers.

What are the pricing options?

We offer Group Tutoring Sessions starting at £25 per month, and highly personalized 1-to-1 Tutoring Sessions starting at £35 per month. You can read more details on the "Our Services" and "Terms & Conditions" pages.

What if my question isn't answered here?

We are always happy to help! Please visit our "Contact Us" page to send us a direct message, and a member of our teaching team will get back to you as soon as possible.
